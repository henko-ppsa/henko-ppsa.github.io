Who we are

About Us

Built on partnership, expertise, and a commitment to your success

Why We Exist

For years, Europe's best processing and packaging technology suppliers struggled to gain ground in Africa. The issue was never equipment quality — it was the lack of a committed local partner. PPSA bridges that gap.

What We Do

We're a family-owned company representing leading manufacturers — Pattyn, Gerstenberg Services, API Schmidt-Bretten, and Daniatech — across Sub-Saharan Africa, matching clients with solutions that fit their exact needs.

How We Work

We grow when you grow. Our approach is rooted in understanding your process, building lasting partnerships, and staying involved long after installation. Success is shared.

Our offering

Technologies

Advanced solutions for food, beverage, and industrial processing

Bag-in-Box Packaging
